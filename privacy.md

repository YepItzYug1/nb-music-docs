 Privacy Policy for NB Music

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NB Music ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Discord bot and related services. By inviting or using NB Music in your Discord server, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect
To function properly and provide music playback services, NB Music processes and stores a minimal amount of data. We do not collect real-life personal details (such as your full name, physical address, or payment details).

A. Automatically Processed Data
- Discord Identifiers: Server (Guild) IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, and User IDs required to route audio, verify permissions, and respond to commands.
- Command Usage & Logs: Temporary logs of executed commands to detect errors, prevent spam, and maintain operational stability.

B. User-Provided Preferences
- Custom Settings: Server prefixes, volume defaults, DJ role configurations, and saved playlists associated with your Discord Server ID or User ID.

C. Voice Data
- Audio Streaming: NB Music joins voice channels to transmit/play audio only. The Bot does not record, store, transmit, or listen to user voice conversations.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:
- Providing core bot features (playing music, managing playback queues, saving server settings).
- Preventing abuse, rate-limit violations, and automated spam attacks.
- Debugging errors and improving bot stability and performance.

3. Data Retention and Storage
- Preference Data: Server settings and saved custom playlists are retained in our database as long as the Bot remains in your server or until a data deletion request is made.
- Temporary Logs: System logs and temporary operational caches are automatically purged on a rolling basis.
- Server Removal: If NB Music is kicked or removed from a server, stored settings for that server may be periodically purged during routine database maintenance.

4. Third-Party Services
NB Music interacts with external services to fetch and stream audio metadata:
- Discord API: Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy (https://discord.com/privacy).
- Third-Party Music & API Providers: When streaming content, search queries or stream URLs are processed to retrieve audio, but no personal Discord user data is shared with external music sources.

We do not sell, trade, or rent any stored user or server data to third parties.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion
You have the right to request the removal of any data associated with your Discord account or server.
- Data Removal: To request immediate deletion of your stored user preferences or server settings, please contact us via email or join our support server.
- Opt-Out: You can stop all data processing at any time by removing (kicking) NB Music from your Discord server.

6. Children's Privacy
NB Music complies with Discord's Terms of Service and does not knowingly collect data from individuals under the minimum age required by applicable law (13 years of age, or higher depending on local jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates to the Bot or legal requirements. We encourage users to check this policy occasionally for any changes.
